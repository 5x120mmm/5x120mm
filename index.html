About

Independent
by design.

#@$% STUDIOS, also known as BLAH STUDIOS, is an independent creative studio based in Los Angeles, working across photography, film, design, and visual storytelling.

We bring together different creative disciplines to develop ideas and turn them into something tangible.

From commercial productions and photography to graphic design and digital content, we work with brands, businesses, and individuals to create work that feels intentional and original.

Whether we’re developing a concept from the ground up or bringing an existing vision to life, we believe every project deserves its own creative approach.

Our work is driven by curiosity, collaboration, and attention to detail.

Different disciplines.
One creative vision.
