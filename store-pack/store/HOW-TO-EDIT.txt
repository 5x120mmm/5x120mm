DIGITAL GARAGE STORE - HOW TO EDIT
==================================

YOUR FOLDERS (what lives where)
-------------------------------
index.html                  YOUR WHOLE SITE IN ONE FILE: portfolio + the store (the "Digital Garage!" tab).
                            Store settings + products are in the "STORE - EDIT ME" box at the very top of the file.
store/
  hero.jpg                  home page photo (replace the file, keep the name)
  logo.png                  white logo (used on the home page)
  logo-outline.png          white logo with thin outline (used on white pages)
  images/                   PRODUCT PHOTOS  (hoodies, tees, stickers, goods)
  media/
    archive/                drop photos + videos -> show on the ARCHIVE page
    products/               drop photos + videos -> gallery on the PRODUCTS page
    contact/                drop photos + videos -> gallery on the CONTACT page

THE EDIT ME BOX (index.html)
----------------------------
Open index.html on GitHub > pencil icon (Edit) > the box is right at the top.
Keep the "quote marks" and the commas between items. Then Commit changes.

 CHANGE A PRICE        find the product line, change   "price": 90
 ADD A PRODUCT         copy one whole product line, paste it under the last one
                       (put a comma after the line above), change id, name, price.
                       Put its photo in store/images/ named  <id>.jpg
 SOLD OUT A SIZE       "soldOut": ["XL","XXL"]      (all sizes listed = Sold out)
 HIDE (move to Archive) "archived": true
 GOODS / STICKERS      "type": "good"  and  "collection": "goods"  (no sizes: "sizes": [])
 YOUR EMAIL (contact form)   settings > "email": "you@email.com"
                       First message sent triggers a one-time confirmation email
                       from FormSubmit - click the link in it. After that, every
                       message from the contact form lands in your inbox.
 INSTAGRAM / TIKTOK / ARCHIVE TITLE   settings > "instagram", "tiktok", "archiveTitle"

PAYMENTS (cart + checkout)
--------------------------
A) SHOPIFY (cart sends customers to your Shopify checkout)
   1. In Shopify, make each product with its sizes (variants).
   2. Open a variant: the number at the end of the web address is its VARIANT ID.
   3. settings > "shopify": {"domain": "yourstore.myshopify.com"}
   4. On each product add its IDs, e.g.
        "shopify": {"S": 4400001, "M": 4400002, "L": 4400003}
      Products without sizes:  "shopify": {"default": 4400009}
   Cart > Checkout then opens Shopify with the items already in the cart.

B) BIG CARTEL (no cart link exists, so each item links to its Big Cartel page)
   On each product:  "buyLink": "https://yourstore.bigcartel.com/product/name"
   Cart > Checkout then lists a link per item.

C) ONE LINK FOR EVERYTHING (Stripe/PayPal etc.):  settings > "checkoutUrl": "https://..."

PHOTOS + VIDEOS ON PAGES
------------------------
GitHub > open store/media/<page>/ > Add file > Upload files > Commit.
Newest uploads show first (about 10 minutes to appear).
